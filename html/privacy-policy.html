Privacy Policy — Orglam Dashboard

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Orglam Dashboard is an internal operations tool used by Orglam ("we", "us") staff to manage e-commerce orders, warehouse stock, and customer communications across Shopify, Amazon, Noon, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger.

What information we access

  • Customer messages sent to our WhatsApp Business number, Instagram Business account, and Facebook Page — including text, images, and voice notes — so our support staff can view and reply to them in one place.
  • Basic sender profile information made available by the platform (name, phone number, or username) to identify who is messaging us.
  • Order and product data from Shopify, used to let staff create orders directly from a conversation.

How we use this information

Solely to provide customer support and process orders for people who contact our business. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data storage and retention

Messages and order data are stored on our secured backend (Cloudflare) and are only accessible to authorized Orglam staff. Data is retained only as long as needed to support ongoing customer service and business record-keeping.
