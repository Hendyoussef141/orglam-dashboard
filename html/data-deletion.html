Data Deletion Instructions — Orglam Dashboard

Last updated: August 1, 2026

If you have messaged our business on WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook Messenger and would like your conversation data deleted from our systems, you can request deletion at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@orglam.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including the phone number, Instagram username, or Facebook profile you contacted us from.

We will locate and permanently delete the associated conversation history from our systems within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

Contact

Orglam — support@orglam.com
